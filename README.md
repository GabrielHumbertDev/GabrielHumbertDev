## About me

I'm an **HRIS Analyst supporting SAP SuccessFactors** in a global enterprise environment, with a **2:1 BSc (Hons) in Computer Science (Cybersecurity)** from the University of Greenwich and professional software engineering training.

My work sits between **people, business processes and technology**. I support SuccessFactors operations across **16+ countries in EMEA, APAC and North America** — employee data management, reporting, access governance, integrations and UAT — working daily with People Teams, payroll stakeholders, country teams and external consultants.

Alongside HR technology, I build software and AI-enabled tools with **Java, Python, SQL, FastAPI, React and Docker**. I'm most at home translating business challenges into practical, secure and scalable technology solutions — and explaining them clearly to non-technical stakeholders, a skill sharpened by years of premium customer-facing work before moving into tech.

## What I bring

- **SAP SuccessFactors support:** Employee Central, employee data management, Time Off, workflows, scheduled reporting, permissions and system health-check support across a 16+ country landscape.
- **Integration and testing:** SuccessFactors → Active Directory testing, Microsoft Entra and Azure exposure, middleware awareness, QAT/UAT design, reconciliation and defect tracking.
- **Business-to-technical translation:** clear communication across technical and non-technical teams, built on global HRIS support and years of high-end customer-facing experience.
- **Software engineering:** Java, Spring Boot, REST APIs, Python, SQL, JPA/Hibernate, JavaScript, React, Kotlin and automated testing (JUnit 5, Mockito, TDD).
- **AI and data:** local AI applications, machine-learning pipelines, model evaluation, data integrity and responsible use of AI.
- **Security-minded delivery:** secure coding foundations, authentication, data protection, network analysis and cybersecurity awareness.
