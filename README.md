## About me

I am an **HRIS Analyst supporting SAP SuccessFactors** in a global enterprise environment, with a **2:1 BSc (Hons) in Computer Science (Cybersecurity)** and professional software engineering training.

My work sits between **people, business processes and technology**. I support SuccessFactors operations across **16+ countries in EMEA, APAC and North America**, working with employees, managers, People Teams, payroll stakeholders, country teams and external consultants.
